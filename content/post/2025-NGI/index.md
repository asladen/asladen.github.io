I recently had the opportunity to visit the [Norwegian Geotechnical Institute (NGI)](https://www.ngi.no/) in Oslo. It was a highly insightful visit, allowing me to discover their impressive geotechnical facilities and engage in stimulating scientific discussions.

During my visit, I also had the pleasure of giving a seminar, sharing our latest research and exchanging ideas with the NGI team.

A special thank you to [Dylan Mikesell](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oy-AAHIAAAAJ&hl=en) for hosting me and for the valuable discussions.
