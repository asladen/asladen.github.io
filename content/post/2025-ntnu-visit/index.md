I have the pleasure of visiting the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in Trondheim as part of the SEASOUNDS project.

NTNU is a key player in developing Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) applications for ocean science and is a vital contributor to the SEASOUNDS network. The visit was is a great opportunity to discuss ongoing research, exchange ideas, and plan future collaborations to push the boundaries of what we can achieve with fiber optic sensing in the marine environment.

A huge thank you to Professor Martin Landrø and his colleagues for their warm hospitality and for hosting a productive and inspiring visit.
