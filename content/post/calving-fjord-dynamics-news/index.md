We are excited to announce that our latest paper, "Calving-driven fjord dynamics resolved by seafloor fibre sensing," has been published in *Nature*.

This study uses seafloor fiber optic sensing to reveal how iceberg calving amplifies submarine melt, providing new insights into ice-ocean interactions.

You can find the publication [here](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09347-7)
