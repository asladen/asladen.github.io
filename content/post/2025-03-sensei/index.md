**SENSEI (Smart European Networks for Sensing the Environment and Internet quality)** is a project funded by the European Union, aimed at developing devices and technologies to turn the global telecommunication fibre network into a pervasive distributed sensor, while also making the network more resilient and efficient. CNRS and Geoazur are part of this project to investigate the potential of new fiber technologies to monitor the enviroment.

On **March 10th, 2025**, SENSEI will be presented to colleagues, stakeholders, and potential collaborators in a 2.5-hour online workshop. The event will feature:

*   Technical talks from Consortium members on the research achievements that form the foundation of SENSEI.
*   A comprehensive overview of the SENSEI project's goals and ambitions.
*   An open discussion and Q&A session.

Participation is free, but registration is required.
