We are proud to share that the PhD research of our student, **Amine Mohammedi**, has been featured in the online magazine **Oceans Connectés**.

The article, titled "Les câbles sous-marins à fibre optique transformés en sentinelles de l’océan" (Underwater fiber optic cables transformed into ocean sentinels), highlights Amine's work on using Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) as a new frontier for continuous and real-time ocean monitoring.

This feature underscores the innovative potential of using existing telecommunication infrastructure to better understand our oceans.

You can read the full article (in French) here: [Oceans Connectés](https://oceansconnectes.org/les-cables-sous-marins-a-fibre-optique-transformes-en-sentinelles-de-locean/).
