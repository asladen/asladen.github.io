We are delighted to welcome Dr. [Chu-Fang (Kim) Yang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kim-chu-fang-yang-470179240), who is joining our research group as a Postdoctoral Fellow.

Kim's research will focus on the characterization of seafloor DAS response at low frequencies. Her work is supported by the CNRS RI² project “D-LIGHT”.

We look forward to her contributions to the team!
